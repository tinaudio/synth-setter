pyFDN sketch flow in the browser

Upload an impulse response. The page extracts its mel spectrogram and reverb sketch, samples the flow with ONNX Runtime Web, renders the predicted FDN with FaustWasm, and scores the render against the upload. Nothing leaves the browser.
